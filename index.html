Frequently Asked Questions

Where's the return Policy?
Because each piece in our collection is unique and one-of-a-kind, all sales are generally final. However, we want you to love what you bring into your home if an item arrives damaged in transit or significantly different from its description, we’ll make it right.
What conditions are your items in?
Every piece at Great Fits Labels is carefully inspected before listing. Because our inventory is second-hand, minor signs of previous love (like light surface wear or gentle patina) are normal and part of the charm. We note any specific scuffs, scratches, or imperfections clearly in the product description and highlight them in our close-up photos so there are never any surprises.
When will I recieve my order?
Delivery time depends on the size of your items and the location combining our preparation time with transit time.Once your order is dispatched, you’ll automatically receive a shipping confirmation with your tracking number and live delivery updates. If you haven't received tracking details after 2 business days, check your spam folder or contact us with your order number.
