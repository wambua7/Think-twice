# Great Fits Labels

>All your items brought at your door step.

![Licens: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green)
![HTML5](https://img.shields.io/badge/HTML5-semantic-e34f26?logo==html5&logoColor=white)

__Great Fit Labels__ is an interactive product showcase page for an online second hand store that displays a variety of goods that users can browse through and add items in a shopping cart that you can self checkout all via the internet.

## The problem we're solving

> "Bringing your desired items to your door step at the comfort of your phone.This saves the long hours spent window shopping and the logistics hassle.

## Features

**For customers**
1. Browse different items available.
2. Click to add to cart.
3. Track your order.
